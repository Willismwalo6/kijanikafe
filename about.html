About Kijani Kafe

A garden restaurant and bar in Milimani, Nakuru.

Our story

Kijani means green in Swahili, and the garden is the heart of the place.

Kijani Kafe is a bar and restaurant on Menengai Drive in Milimani, Nakuru. We serve African and international dishes, including burgers, chicken, fish, steak and chips, alongside a full bar.

Guests come for relaxed outdoor dining, family outings and gatherings with friends. We also take table reservations and enquiries for celebrations, and we deliver.
