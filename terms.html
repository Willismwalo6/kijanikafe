Terms

Orders, delivery, bookings and alcohol sales.

Last updated: October 2026

Orders

Orders sent through this website are requests. An order is accepted only when Kijani Kafe confirms it on WhatsApp or by phone. Prices are in Kenya shillings and may change. Some items may be unavailable.

Delivery

Delivery is available on request. The delivery fee and time are confirmed with you before we dispatch.

Reservations and events

Booking and event forms send a request only. A table or event date is reserved once we confirm it. We may ask you to confirm again or may release a table if guests are late without notice.

Alcohol

Alcohol is sold only to guests aged 18 and over. We may ask for identification.

Menu and photos

Photos are for illustration. Dishes may differ slightly.
