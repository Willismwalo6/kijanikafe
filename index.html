A garden to spend the day in

Kijani Kafe sits on Menengai Drive in Milimani, with a green lawn, shaded tables and a covered veranda. It is a place for meals, family outings and time with friends.

There is outdoor seating for groups, a bar for drinks and cocktails, and space for celebrations. Ask us about private events.

Good to know

What are your opening hours?

We are open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Happy Hour runs Monday to Friday, 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM.

Can I book a table?

Yes. Send a request on the reservations page with your date, time and number of guests. Your table is reserved once we confirm it on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver?

Yes, delivery is available. Choose Delivery when you order online and add your address. We confirm the delivery fee on WhatsApp.

How does online ordering work?

Pick your dishes, enter your details and press Send order on WhatsApp. Your order is ready to send in WhatsApp, and it is confirmed when we reply.

Can I host a birthday or group event?

Yes, we welcome celebrations and group bookings. Send details on the events page and we will reply with availability.

Is there outdoor seating?

Yes. You can sit out on the garden lawn or under the covered veranda.

Do you serve alcohol?

Yes, we have a full bar with beer, cider, spirits, whisky, cocktails and wine. Alcohol is sold only to guests aged 18 and over.
